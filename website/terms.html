Beta Terms of Use

For the current MyIdealBody AI development prototype.

1. Beta access and acceptance

This beta is intended only for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. By using the public website or the signed Google Play Internal or Closed testing build, you acknowledge that this is unfinished evaluation software. If you do not meet the age requirement or do not agree with these terms and the Privacy Policy, do not use the beta.

2. What the beta does

The website can preview a selected meal photo and create a guided calorie/protein range from user-selected meal and portion information. The current public website does not visually recognize the photo. The Android safe default generates a fixed sample result entirely on-device without sending the selected photo or an analysis request. That demo result cannot be saved to the diary.

Features, data formats, limits, and availability may change or stop without notice during development.

3. Nutrition and health limitations

  • All calories, protein, portions, confidence values, and food labels are estimates.
  • A photo cannot reveal exact weight, recipe, oil, coconut milk, sugar, sauces, allergens, contamination, or every ingredient.
  • The beta is not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, laboratory measurement, dietetic service, or substitute for a qualified professional.
  • Seek professional advice for health conditions, pregnancy, allergies, eating disorders, medication interactions, or therapeutic diets.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Use only photos and data you are permitted to use.
  • Keep people, documents, locations, and other unnecessary personal information out of meal photos.
  • Review and correct every estimate before relying on or saving it.
  • Do not probe, overload, bypass limits, reverse engineer a hosted service, submit malicious files, or use the beta unlawfully.
  • Do not represent beta output as clinically validated or guaranteed.

5. Photos and content

You keep any rights you have in content you select. Under current default behavior, the public website keeps the photo in browser memory and the Android demo keeps the selected photo on-device. This does not grant permission for model training. If a future build asks to upload a real photo, a revised disclosure and policy must apply before that feature is enabled.

6. Accounts, payments, and subscriptions

The current beta has no user account system. Prices shown in DEMO_MODE are static launch previews or targets, not an active offer, and the demo does not initialize Play Billing. A separately enabled non-demo build may display Play-localized product details only after authenticated server verification is configured; until then, checkout fails closed. Ketentuan Penggunaan Beta

Untuk prototipe pengembangan MyIdealBody AI saat ini.

1. Akses dan persetujuan beta

Beta ini hanya ditujukan bagi pengguna dewasa berusia 18 tahun atau lebih dan tidak ditujukan kepada anak-anak. Dengan menggunakan situs publik atau build pengujian Internal atau Tertutup Google Play yang ditandatangani, kamu memahami bahwa perangkat lunak ini belum selesai dan hanya untuk evaluasi. Jika belum memenuhi batas usia atau tidak menyetujui ketentuan ini dan Kebijakan Privasi, jangan gunakan beta.

2. Fungsi beta

Situs dapat menampilkan pratinjau foto makanan dan membuat rentang kalori/protein terpandu dari jenis makanan dan porsi yang dipilih pengguna. Situs publik saat ini tidak mengenali foto secara visual. Pengaturan aman bawaan Android menghasilkan contoh hasil tetap sepenuhnya di perangkat tanpa mengirim foto pilihan atau permintaan analisis. Hasil demo tersebut tidak dapat disimpan ke catatan makanan.

Fitur, format data, batas penggunaan, dan ketersediaan dapat berubah atau dihentikan selama pengembangan.

3. Batasan gizi dan kesehatan

  • Semua kalori, protein, porsi, tingkat keyakinan, dan label makanan merupakan estimasi.
  • Foto tidak dapat menunjukkan berat, resep, minyak, santan, gula, saus, alergen, kontaminasi, atau semua bahan secara tepat.
  • Beta bukan perangkat medis, diagnosis, perawatan, pengukuran laboratorium, layanan ahli gizi, atau pengganti tenaga profesional.
  • Mintalah saran profesional untuk kondisi kesehatan, kehamilan, alergi, gangguan makan, interaksi obat, atau diet terapi.

4. Tanggung jawab pengguna

  • Gunakan hanya foto dan data yang berhak kamu gunakan.
  • Hindari memasukkan wajah, dokumen, lokasi, dan informasi pribadi lain yang tidak diperlukan dalam foto makanan.
  • Periksa dan koreksi setiap estimasi sebelum dipakai atau disimpan.
