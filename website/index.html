Good questions, direct answers

Before you point the camera.

Can a photo measure exact calories?

No. A photo cannot reveal exact weight or every ingredient. MyIdealBody AI is designed to return a useful range, explain uncertainty, and let you edit the result.

Is the app available on Google Play?

Not yet. The repository is a development MVP for closed-beta preparation. A public paid release needs production validation and Play setup first.

Does it analyze live video?

No. This website uses one still photo. With no production API configured, the photo stays in your browser and the estimate comes from your selections—not visual recognition.

Is this medical or dietetic advice?

No. It provides general wellness estimates only and is not a diagnosis, treatment, laboratory measurement, or substitute for a qualified professional.
