### Full description — under 4,000 characters

```text
MyIdealBody AI is an early closed beta for adults to review the interface for a future meal-nutrition tool.

IMPORTANT BETA NOTICE
This test build uses one fixed sample result. It does not inspect or upload your meal photo, contact an analysis service, or provide real AI food recognition. The sample is created on your device and cannot be saved to the diary. Do not use it to make dietary or health decisions.

CAMERA AND PHOTO BEHAVIOR
Photo capture requires a compatible Android camera. You can instead select a supported JPEG, PNG, or WebP image with the system picker. This closed-test demo does not inspect the image. It uses one still photo for the interface flow and does not continuously measure calories from video or use the camera as a medical sensor.

WHAT TESTERS CAN TRY
- Capture a meal photo or choose one from the gallery
- Review fixed demonstration ranges for calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat
- Edit sample portions and review hidden-ingredient assumptions
- Verify that the demo result is clearly labeled and cannot be saved as a real meal
- Use the app in English or Bahasa Indonesia
- Test permission denial, offline, retry, and accessibility behavior

WHY RESULTS ARE EDITABLE
A photo cannot reliably reveal exact portions or hidden oil, sugar, sauces, or coconut milk. The app therefore uses ranges and asks you to review assumptions instead of claiming exact measurements.

CLOSED-BETA ACCESS AND PRICING
This build is for invited testers. No paid subscription or production entitlement is offered in this beta. If paid access is introduced later, checkout will show the localized price and billing period supplied by Google Play.

HEALTH DISCLAIMER
This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Nutrition values are estimates for general wellness only. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

By joining the closed test, you agree to use only non-sensitive test photos and report inaccurate, confusing, or broken behavior through the tester feedback channel.
```

MyIdealBody AI adalah beta tertutup tahap awal bagi pengguna dewasa untuk meninjau antarmuka alat gizi makanan yang direncanakan.

PEMBERITAHUAN PENTING TENTANG BETA
Versi uji ini memakai satu contoh hasil tetap. Versi ini tidak memeriksa atau mengunggah foto makanan Anda, tidak menghubungi layanan analisis, dan belum merupakan pengenalan makanan berbasis AI. Demo menggunakan satu foto diam untuk alur antarmuka dan tidak terus-menerus mengukur kalori dari video maupun menggunakan kamera sebagai sensor medis.

YANG DAPAT DIUJI

## Pricing and publication note

- Keep the Play app **free** during internal and closed testing.
- Do not enable the purchase button while backend verification and production
  entitlements are unavailable.
- The planned prices are not equivalent offers: Rp49,000 and about US$4.99 are
  monthly references, while US$19.99 is the proposed annual launch reference.
- In a paid release, render the localized `ProductDetails` price and billing
  period returned by Google Play. Do not hard-code rupiah or dollar prices in
  the checkout UI.
- Re-review both translations with a native-language reviewer before production.
